Deepr saves links and app deep links so you can open them again in one tap.

Paste a link, share one to Deepr from any app, or scan a QR code. Deepr fetches the title and preview image, and you can add tags and a note. Everything stays on your phone. There's no account and no sign-in.

What you can do:
- Open deep links straight into other apps, not just web pages
- Tag links and filter by one or more tags
- Star favourites and start the app on them
- Sort by newest, most opened, or name
- Pin a link to your home screen as a shortcut
- Keep work and personal links apart with profiles
- Save from the share menu without opening Deepr ("Save silently")
- Manage links from a computer through the built-in local web server and API
- Export to CSV or browser bookmarks (HTML), auto-back up to a folder, or import from CSV and Chrome bookmarks
- Lock the app with your screen lock

Deepr is free and open source: https://github.com/yogeshpaliyal/Deepr
